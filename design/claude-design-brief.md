## Shared brief (paste first)

I'm Davide La Rocca. I want a homepage for davidelarocca.com that works as a distinctive digital
identity, not a conventional portfolio with an unusual theme. It should convey a multidisciplinary
thinker, creator, researcher and builder: intellectual independence, curiosity, strong aesthetic
judgment, and the ability to connect ideas with execution. It must demonstrate creative judgment,
not proclaim it.

**Who I am (facts, use them, do not invent others):**
- Marketing & Communication Manager at Costa Cruises (onboard communication and guest experience).
- Researcher on brand authenticity: co-authored chapter "Authenticity as a Reputation-Building
  Strategy in Underdog Brands", De Gruyter Handbook of Corporate Reputation Management, 2026.
  Earlier theses on authenticity and luxury (LUISS 2024, Lund University 2019, Bologna 2017).
- Builder: Swift Harness, my open-source Claude Code plugin for structured, AI-assisted native
  Apple development (design-system workflow plus automatic quality gates: format, lint, build, test).
- Works across Italian, English, French; lived and studied in Sweden, France, Czechia, Romania.

**Positioning, the three dimensions (identity, not navigation):**
1. Human authenticity, brand strategy, research.
2. AI, software, systems thinking, product building.
3. Culture, design, aesthetics, experimentation.

**Statement to explore (direction, not final copy):** "I care about what feels real. I build what comes next."

**Homepage content:** the statement, a one-line factual bio, two featured pieces (the De Gruyter
chapter and Swift Harness), a quiet way to reach writing (Substack), links (LinkedIn, GitHub,
Substack, Medium, email). Professional background and CV live on a secondary page, one clear link.
Leave room for future experiments, software, writing, photography, music and ventures without
turning the site into unrelated sections.

**Constraints:** static site (GitHub Pages), English only, responsive (design desktop 1440 and
mobile 390 as two designs, not a squeeze), accessible (keyboard, contrast, reduced motion), fast
(no heavy video, self-hosted fonts).

**Avoid:** AI gradients, glowing orbs, glassmorphism, standard SaaS components, portfolio cards,
"hi, I'm" heroes, skill bars, logo walls, empty visionary slogans, a listed CV on the homepage.

**Craft rules (from Benji Taylor's Stageshow skill, applied to this site):**
- Restraint: count the real strings. Real words only where the story is; everything else quiet.
- One focal object per screen; the crop puts the eye where the story is.
- Every motion has a cause. Nothing pops, nothing moves only to decorate.
- Four failure modes to check against: too much, wireframe, boxed (everything in even cards),
  flat (every object the same weight), bland (generic, whatever the brief said).
- Look at the result as the most exacting designer you know: find the one wrong thing, fix it.

Reference for confidence and restraint: benji.org. Take the attitude, not the look.

## Concept 1: The Living Index (favourite, explore in depth)

The homepage is a single index that grows over time. Selection, ordering and scale do the talking:
what is listed, how it is numbered, what is set large. One serif family with optical sizes,
paper and ink, one accent. A reading lens lets the visitor read the index through one of the three
dimensions. No images required.

Explore it in three variations of the same idea, so I can compare nuances, not different concepts:
- **Strict:** almost only type and numbers; the statement is the only large element.
- **Annotated:** marginal notes (sidenotes) carry the context of each entry, like a scholarly edition.
- **Tended:** the index shows time: when an entry was added, last tended, what is in progress.

Show how it holds with only 2 to 3 entries today, and how it reads with 25 entries in two years
(writing, photography, music, ventures added without new sections).

## Concept 4: The Logbook

Built on the fact that I work at sea: the site as a ship's log of thinking. Dated entries, positions
replaced by the three dimensions, a calm maritime typographic system (no clichés: no anchors, waves,
nautical clip art). The log is where future writing, photography and music naturally land.
Keep the restraint of the Living Index: this can be read as the Index with time as its spine.

## Concept 2 (revised): The Creative Laboratory, serif + mono

The homepage is a working notebook: each work is an experiment with question, method and outcome,
margin notes beside it, the Feel ↔ Build statement at the top, the Swift Harness terminal preview.
Same playful intelligence as before, but set in an editorial serif plus a nerdy mono, closer to a
scholar's lab book than to handwriting. Also show the graft: the Living Index carrying these ideas
as details, so I can compare "Laboratory on its own" vs "Index with lab details".

## Concept 5: The Monograph

The homepage as the opening spread of a printed monograph or exhibition catalogue: a title page,
a short essay-like introduction, plates for the two featured works, a colophon. Scrolling turns
pages with editorial rhythm. Print-grade typography, generous margins, footnotes as interaction.
Keep the restraint of the Living Index: this can be read as the Index set as a book.
